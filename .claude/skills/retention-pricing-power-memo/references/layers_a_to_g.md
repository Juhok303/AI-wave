# Universe Filter + Layers A–G

## Universe Filter (run before anything else)

Judge whether this investment philosophy is even applicable to the company.

**Good fit (Framework Fit: High/Medium likely):**
- 상장기업, B2C 중심
- 반복구매 또는 반복사용 구조
- Subscription / Membership / Platform / Consumer Brand / Repeat Purchase 모델
- 고객 유지율(retention) 또는 반복구매를 직접·간접으로 추정 가능
- 가격 또는 Product Mix 변화를 관찰 가능
- 예: 구독 서비스, 디지털 플랫폼, 멤버십, 소비재, 리테일, 반복구매형 브랜드, 일부 B2B2C 플랫폼

**Poor fit (Framework Fit: Low):**
- 순수 B2B, 프로젝트성 수주기업, 원자재 Commodity, 일회성 구매 중심 내구재,
  가격 규제가 강한 산업, Retention/Cohort를 전혀 추정할 수 없는 기업

Output `Framework Fit: High / Medium / Low` first. If Low, explain plainly why the philosophy doesn't
apply well and do not force the full 14-section memo — a short explanation is the correct, complete
output for a Low-fit company.

## Layer A — Consumer Demand

**Question:** 이 카테고리/서비스에 대한 소비자 수요가 구조적으로 확대되는가?

Look at: 시장 성장률, 소비 빈도 변화, 카테고리 침투율, 세대별 Adoption, 신규 사용 Occasion, 인접
카테고리로부터 지출 이전 가능성, TAM 확장성.

Prefer hard numbers: Category YoY Growth, Penetration %, Customer/User Growth, Purchase Frequency,
Spending per User, Search/App/Web Traffic Trend.

Classify: **Bull Signal / Neutral / Bear Signal**. Market growth alone is never sufficient for
investment attractiveness — it's an input to later layers, not a conclusion.

## Layer B — Product / Brand Strength

**Question:** 소비자가 경쟁 제품 대신 이 제품/서비스를 반복해서 선택하는 이유가 있는가?

Look at: 제품 차별성, 브랜드 선호, 고객 만족, NPS, 리뷰, 반복구매, Premiumization, 가격 프리미엄,
Product Mix.

Required evidence types: Review Rating, Review Volume, Repeat Purchase, NPS, Premium SKU Mix,
Competitive Price Premium. Abstract claims like "브랜드가 강하다" are prohibited without a number
backing them.

## Layer C — Customer Economics (one of the two most important layers)

**Question:** 높은 Retention이 실제 경제적 가치로 이어지고 있는가?

**Retention:** Cohort Retention (3/6/12/24-month), Retention Curve shape, recent cohort improvement,
Repeat Purchase Rate, Purchase Frequency.

**Churn:** Churn %, churn change around price increases, churn change when competitors run
promotions.

**Customer Economics:** CAC, LTV, LTV/CAC, CAC Payback, AOV, ARPU, Contribution Margin.

**Key question to answer explicitly:** Retention이 높기만 한가, 아니면 기업이 이를 실제로
Monetize하고 있는가?

Run the **Pricing Power Test** (see `pricing_power_test.md`) as part of this layer.

## Layer D — Competitive Advantage

**Question:** 이 Pricing Power가 경쟁에 의해 쉽게 무너지는가?

Look at: Switching Cost, Habit, Data Lock-in, Network Effect, Brand Preference, Distribution
Advantage, Scale Economy, Cost Advantage, 경쟁사 가격공세, 신규 진입.

**Strong positive evidence to explicitly check for:** 경쟁사가 가격을 낮췄거나 Promotion을 확대했음에도
해당 기업의 Retention이 유지되는가? Treat this as strong empirical proof of pricing power if found.

## Layer E — Distribution

**Question:** 이 고객경제성 우위를 더 많은 소비자에게 확장할 수 있는가?

Look at: Owned Channel %, Organic Traffic %, Paid Traffic %, Marketplace Dependency, Store/Region
Expansion, App/Web Traffic, Distribution Density, 플랫폼 의존도. Also check whether CAC rises or falls
as distribution expands.

## Layer F — Financial Translation

**Question:** 좋은 고객 지표가 실제로 좋은 회계 숫자로 바뀌고 있는가?

Decompose Revenue Growth into **Volume + Price + Mix** wherever possible.

Margins to track: Gross Margin, Contribution Margin, EBITDA Margin, Operating Margin, FCF Margin.

Operating Leverage check: is **Revenue Growth > Opex Growth** sustained?

Capital Efficiency: ROIC, FCF Conversion, Working Capital, Capex Intensity.

## Layer G — Variant Perception

Covered fully in `variant_perception.md`. Do not just find "market is wrong" — verify the market is
genuinely unaware, not just that you disagree with a known consensus view.

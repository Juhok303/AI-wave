# Layer G — Variant Perception

The goal is not to find "a good company" — it's to find where **the market is wrong**.

Write this in exactly this structure:

### What the Market Believes
현재 컨센서스가 기업을 어떻게 보고 있는가? Identify the market's implicit assumptions about:
Revenue Growth, User/Volume Growth, Margin, Price, Mix, TAM, Multiple.

### What We Believe
우리의 판단은 무엇인가— specifically how it differs from the market's assumptions above.

### Why the Market May Be Wrong
Possible reasons, e.g.:
- 데이터 공개 시차
- Retention이 재무제표 밖에 존재
- Price/Mix 영향 과소평가
- 셀사이드가 Volume 중심으로 모델링
- 최근 가격인상의 Cohort 효과가 컨센서스에 미반영

### Evidence
The concrete data that supports our differentiated view (with value + period + source).

### Recognition Trigger
When is the market likely to realize this?

## Mandatory self-check: classify the variant view

Classify what you found into exactly one of these six categories:

1. 시장이 이미 알고 있는 좋은 점
2. 시장이 알지만 중요도를 낮게 보는 점
3. 아직 충분히 인식하지 못한 점
4. 시장이 잘못 해석하는 점
5. 재무제표에 아직 나타나지 않은 선행지표
6. Valuation에 반영되지 않은 Optionality

**Category 1 does NOT count as a Variant View.** If everything you found falls into category 1, say
so plainly — this materially weakens the Core Thesis and should push the Variant Perception score
toward 0 (see Consensus Gate in `scorecard_and_verdict.md`).

Before claiming a variant view, explicitly ask: **"정말 시장이 모르는가?"** and justify the answer.
